Screenshot artifacts are generated locally under ignored `screenshots/` and reproducible with `node scripts/validate-shadow-receiver-browser.js`.

Performance answer: the receiver itself is one draw/lighting surface, but native shadow casters require a depth/shadow-map render of every visible caster each update plus receiver sampling; the current note/obstacle fake shadows are one simple transparent geometry item per target and do not require their GLBs to cast. Adding potentially dozens of beats and translucent moving wall GLBs increases shadow-pass draw calls, GPU bandwidth, and special-case alpha/depth handling, especially on mobile. No measured FPS comparison was run; recommend retaining existing fake beat/wall shadows unless a separate representative scene benchmark and visual test demonstrates acceptable cost and better readability.
